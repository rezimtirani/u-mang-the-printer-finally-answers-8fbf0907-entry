One line from the office printer, finally answering back after years of paper jams, cryptic error codes and thumps: it is holding your boarding pass, your tax return and your resignation letter — and one more thump turns all three into confetti.

> I have your boarding pass, your tax return, and your resignation letter. One more thump and all three come out as confetti.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the comeback to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 240 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one line, in the printer's own weary, literal, faintly passive-aggressive voice.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
